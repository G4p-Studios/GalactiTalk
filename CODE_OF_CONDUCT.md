# Code of conduct

GalactiTalk's community should be welcoming, constructive, and safe.

Participants must treat others with respect, accept good-faith feedback, avoid
harassment and discriminatory behavior, and protect private information. This
applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or limit
participation when behavior harms the community. Serious or repeated violations
may result in a permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owners. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers will avoid
conflicts of interest when handling a report.
